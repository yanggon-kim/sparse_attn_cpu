Just 20 years ago, many archaeologists believed there was a “human revolution” 40,000-50,000 years ago during which modern behaviours such as symbolism, innovation and art suddenly arose. This was thought to have enabled a major shift in cognitive organisation and probably the advent of complex language. At the time, the earliest modern human fossils had been found in Africa and dated to some 100,000 years ago, leaving a gap between the emergence of anatomically modern humans and behaviourally modern humans. NEWLINE_CHAR NEWLINE_CHAR This gap in the development suggested that we only achieved “modernity” as our species migrated out of Africa and into the rest of the Old World. NEWLINE_CHAR NEWLINE_CHAR Not all researchers supported the view that modernity arose outside of Africa.
